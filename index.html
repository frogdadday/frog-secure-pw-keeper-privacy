Frog Secure PW Keeper Privacy Policy

Free. Simple. Respectful.

Effective date: September 23, 2026

The short version

Frog Secure PW Keeper is an offline password safe. It does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information. It has no ads, analytics, accounts, subscriptions, cloud service, or internet permission.

Information stored on your device

The passwords, usernames, websites, and titles you enter are stored only inside the app's private storage on your Android device. The vault is strongly encrypted. Your master password is never stored. Android's hardware-backed Keystore is used when the device supports it.

Network access and tracking

The app does not request Android's INTERNET permission and cannot connect to our servers. We operate no account system or server for the app. The app contains no advertising, analytics, telemetry, tracking SDKs, or social features.

Clipboard

When you choose to copy a username or password, the app marks the clipboard item as sensitive and schedules it to clear after 20 seconds. Android may delay clipboard access while the app is in the background, and keyboards or other apps may keep their own clipboard history. The app cannot control data retained by another app.

Encrypted backup files

Backups are created only when you manually export one. The backup remains encrypted with the master password protecting that vault. Android's file picker lets you choose where it is saved. If you choose a cloud-storage provider, that provider may upload the encrypted file under its own privacy policy. Frog Secure PW Keeper does not perform that upload.

Android backups and permissions

Automatic Android backup is disabled for the app. No storage permission is requested; manual import and export use Android's system file picker, which grants access only to the file you select. The app requests no runtime permissions.

Data deletion

Deleting an entry removes it from the active vault. Uninstalling the app or clearing its app data removes the local vault from that device. Encrypted backup files you exported remain wherever you saved them and must be deleted there by you.

Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app's privacy behavior changes, this policy will be updated before the changed version is released. The effective date above will also be updated.

Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue in this policy's public GitHub repository.
